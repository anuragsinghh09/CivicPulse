About CivicPulse

A transparent civic service portal

CivicPulse is designed to support the everyday civic needs of a city by giving citizens a simple way to report issues such as potholes, garbage, water leakage, street-light failure, and drainage conditions.

The platform keeps complaint records structured and traceable, allowing administrators to verify actionable issues, assign them to departments, and monitor resolution progress until completion.

The result is a practical, role-based complaint workflow that balances citizen visibility with controlled administrative action.

Objectives

  • Improve citizen complaint reporting.
  • Standardize complaint review and assignment.
  • Track case progress with status history.
  • Support citizen feedback after resolution.
